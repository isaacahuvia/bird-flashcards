How it works

The bird list comes from eBird reports for your trip dates across past years. Photos are iNaturalist featured photos and favorited research-grade photos at least 1000 px wide. Audio is xeno-canto recordings graded A (B only where A is scarce), 3 seconds to 3 minutes long, with at most one other bird in the background. Birds with nothing that meets those bars are skipped. Range, habitat and behavior come from each bird's Wikipedia article.

Review: new birds come in a few at a time, most common first. A bird you miss comes back after about 3 cards, then after 6, then 15. Once you get it right 3 times running, it comes back a day later, then 3 days, a week, two weeks. Learned means right 4 times running, with the last at least a day after the others. A miss, or tapping Reveal, starts the bird over.

On a phone, add this page to your home screen, then download decks from there; the home-screen app keeps its own downloads and progress.
