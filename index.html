How it works

The bird list comes from eBird reports for your trip dates across past years. Photos are iNaturalist featured photos and favorited research-grade photos at least 1000 px wide, without egg, nest or dead-bird photos. Audio is xeno-canto recordings graded A (B only where A is scarce), 3 seconds to 3 minutes, with at most one other bird in the background.

Clues. Range: where on this trip the bird is reported on at least 5% of days (Tr Trinidad, To Tobago; Fo La Fortuna, Mo Monteverde, Do San Gerardo de Dota). Common, uncommon, rare: reported on 40%+, 10–40%, or under 10% of days in the area you picked. Habitat and where seen: from the bird's family and its Wikipedia article; they're automatic and can be off for some birds.

Levels. Every bird starts as Familiar, answered by picking from four names that look unlike it. After 2 right in a row, the wrong answers become similar birds (same family where possible). Recognized: 5 right in a row among similar birds, spread over at least 2 days. Recognized birds are then asked by recall: you name the bird yourself, tap Reveal, and mark Got it or Missed it. Known (learned): 5 recalls right in a row over at least 2 days. A miss drops a bird one level. Until a bird is Known it comes back within a few cards and then daily; Known birds come back after 1, 3, 7 and 14 days.

Drills. When every Common bird in an area is Known, gold Drills unlock for that area; the icon upgrades when every Regular bird, and then every Uncommon bird, is Known too. Drills go through all your Known birds there in random order, by recall. A miss is a strike; 3 strikes and the bird drops back to Recognized.

On a phone, add this page to your home screen and use it from there; the home-screen app keeps its own downloads and progress.
